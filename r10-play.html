R10-P0 is a small synthetic fixture test, not a released game. The order, prices, stamina, card rewards, free dungeon return and one permanent test crop are not approved production balance. The 3D farm uses generated placeholder parcels and crops, not an authored asset pipeline. Reload/Reset starts from revision 0; there are no saved accounts, procedural worlds, Railway gameplay services or live co-op. The WebGL views are projections of the same authoritative in-memory reducer and can be checked against the semantic SVG/JSON.
